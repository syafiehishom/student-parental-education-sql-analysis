# 📊 Key Insights from The strongest indicator of high-GPA students query

## 1. Absences as the Strongest Predictor
Absenteeism shows the strongest negative relationship with academic performance.  
High-performing students have an average of **2.9 absences**, while low-performing students have **21.0 absences** — nearly **7 times higher**.  

This suggests that consistent attendance is a critical factor in achieving high academic performance.

---

## 2. Study Time Has Moderate Impact
High-performing students study an average of **11.7 hours per week**, compared to **9.1 hours** for low-performing students.  

While there is a positive relationship between study time and GPA, the difference is relatively small compared to the gap in absences, indicating that study time alone is not the strongest driver of performance.

---

## 3. Parental Support Positively Influences Performance
Parental support increases steadily with GPA:
- High GPA: **2.52**
- Medium GPA: **2.14**
- Low GPA: **2.01**

This suggests that a supportive home environment contributes to better academic outcomes.

---

## 4. Limited Impact of Tutoring
Tutoring participation shows only a slight increase among high-performing students:
- High GPA: **0.46**
- Medium GPA: **0.29**
- Low GPA: **0.27**

This indicates that tutoring plays a smaller role compared to attendance and study habits.

---

## 🎯 Overall Conclusion
Academic success is more strongly associated with **consistent attendance and supportive environments** than with tutoring or study time alone.
